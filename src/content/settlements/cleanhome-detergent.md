Consumers who purchased CleanHome Liquid Laundry Detergent (100 oz or larger) between 2020 and 2024 may claim compensation.

You can claim $5 per bottle. You do not need proof of purchase to claim up to 5 bottles ($25 max). Claims for more than 5 bottles require receipts.
