Consumers who purchased beef for personal use between August 2014 and December 2019 may be eligible for a payment.

This settlement resolves claims that major beef producers (like Tyson and Cargill) conspired to limit the supply of beef and increase prices. Claims can be submitted without proof of purchase, with payouts varying based on the number of claims.
